Demand Lab

Demand describes how much of a good buyers want at each price. When the price falls, people buy more. That is the law of demand, and it makes the curve slope down. Slide the price to move along the curve, then change what buyers care about and watch the whole curve shift.

Elasticity and revenue

How strongly buyers react to price decides whether a price rise brings in more money or less.

Demand here is a straight line: Qd = a − b·P, where a collects everything except the good's own price. Real demand curves bend, and the effects of income, substitutes and tastes differ from good to good. Numbers are illustrative.
